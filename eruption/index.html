How to play

  1. Erupt. Your ball pays its landing-bay multiplier and chips away at a building. Each bet adds 3% of its amount to the Tower Pot.
  2. Bring down the tower. It takes 15 hits. Other buildings stay as rubble; those bays still pay normally.
  3. Collect the pot. Tower collapse pauses new launches. Balls in flight finish, then the Tower Pot is paid once. Auto stops at the city finale.
  4. Watch the rebuild. Cranes and crews restore the city. The next city starts automatically once rebuilding finishes. No new bet is placed until you erupt.

Gold demolition ball (5% chance). Pays 2× the normal bay payout and destroys any standing building in one hit. Hitting the clock tower triggers the Tower Pot finale. The Tower Pot itself is not doubled. Hits on rubble still pay double, without another destruction bonus.

Only the clock tower awards a demolition bonus. Warps transport balls between pegs without changing landing odds.
